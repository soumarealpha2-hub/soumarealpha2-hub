## My framework

1. Define the business outcome before choosing the tool.
2. Map the current workflow and identify the constraint.
3. Bring users and stakeholders into the decision early.
4. Build the smallest useful operating system or prototype.
5. Measure adoption and business results, then iterate.

The details shared here are limited to information cleared for a public portfolio. No confidential datasets, internal documents, or personal information are included.
