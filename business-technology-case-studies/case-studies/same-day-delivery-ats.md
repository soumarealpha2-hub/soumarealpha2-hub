# Recruiting Operations and Applicant Tracking System

## Context

A delivery company needed to hire reliably while expanding its fleet. Applicant information, interview scheduling, and status tracking lived across disconnected steps, which slowed decisions and made follow-up difficult.

## Problem

The recruiting process needed a single, practical workflow that a busy operations manager could use without adding administrative burden.

## My role

I mapped the hiring process, designed the screening flow, organized applicant stages, and connected the experience to the company's website. I treated the ATS as an operating system for the hiring team, not just a form.

## Approach

1. Documented the path from application to screening, interview, decision, and onboarding.
2. Defined clear applicant stages and required fields.
3. Added scheduling logic so qualified applicants could move forward quickly.
4. Created status visibility for the manager and reduced repeated manual follow-up.
5. Connected hiring capacity to the broader fleet-growth plan.

## Results

- Reduced applicant screening time by **68%**
- Cut time-to-hire from **14 days to 5**
- Supported **35% fleet growth**
- Contributed to fleet-technology deployment across **100+ vehicles**
- Helped improve dispatch accuracy by **23%**
- Designed workflows that saved **20+ staff members approximately 12 hours per week each**

## What I learned

The best workflow automation respects the pace of the people using it. A technically complete system can still fail if stages are confusing, ownership is unclear, or the team cannot see what needs attention next.
