# BISSAPIO Product and Go-to-Market

## Context

BISSAPIO is a West African hibiscus beverage concept built around cultural storytelling, product design, and community impact.

## Problem

The early-stage brand needed to improve operating execution while developing a product story that could resonate with customers, partners, and investors.

## My role

As a co-founder, I worked across product design, packaging, business development, marketing, and the investor narrative. The work required moving between creative decisions and practical operating constraints.

## Approach

1. Connected the product story to the history and cultural meaning of hibiscus beverages.
2. Improved packaging and the way the brand communicated its purpose.
3. Reviewed operating steps to reduce handoff delays.
4. Shaped a clearer business-development and investor presentation.
5. Used feedback to refine both the product and the growth story.

## Results

- Reduced operating lead times by **15%**
- Helped develop product packaging and brand positioning
- Presented the venture narrative to **10+ investors**
- Built experience connecting cultural authenticity with commercial execution

## What I learned

Strong go-to-market work is not just promotion. Product design, supply execution, positioning, and customer trust all reinforce one another. The brand becomes stronger when those decisions tell the same story.
